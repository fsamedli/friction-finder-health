# Data specification (synthetic)

Generated by `data/generate.py`, seed 42, on every run. Fictional company: Lanford Health (fictional), a regional hospital system (three hospitals, Day and Night shifts). Nothing here is real, and nothing is patient-level: every operating metric is a count at unit, shift, or site level. The shape follows a survey-vendor team export (team x wave x item aggregates) so that a real export could replace the survey tables without code changes.

## How the numbers are made

- Survey: 12 items, 1 to 5. Each team has a baseline near 3.85 per item; each employee has a fixed offset (SD 0.2); each answer adds noise (SD 0.5) and is rounded to 1 to 5. Response rate about 85% per wave.
- Tenure: bands under_1, 1_to_3, 3_to_10, over_10 with shares [0.22, 0.3, 0.33, 0.15]. Under-one-year employees score 0.35 higher on every item (the year-one honeymoon effect Gallup described on Sept 14, 2026).
- Medication passes: daily scheduled doses per site, shift, and hour band (Poisson), with a flat 8% late-dose rate (given more than 60 minutes after the scheduled time) except where planted; missed doses Poisson(1.5).
- Staffing: monthly scheduled versus actual RN headcount, about 97% except where planted.
- Comments: one to two generic comments per reportable team-wave, drawn from a small bank, plus the planted ones.
- Team-month operating records (`team_ops_monthly`): absence days, late doses, quits, overtime shifts and injuries per team and month, Poisson around a per-head base rate that moves with the team's engagement (P12), plus the P11 multipliers.

## Checks

- `tests/test_data.py`: every planted pattern is present at the stated strength; no comment comes from a suppressed team-wave; every employee id is unique.
- `tests/test_agent.py`: suppression in SQL, numbers match the truth file, refusals, brief checks, red-team verdicts.
- `evals/`: gold questions scored as a confusion matrix over the planted patterns (found, missed, invented, correctly declined).

## Known limits

- Real survey text is human behaviour; the comment bank is small and the wording is ours (judge Chang, Sept 15, 2026).
- Response bias (only the happy or the angry answer) is not modelled (judges Fullowan and Lederman).
- Two waves only; no Gallup global-database benchmark or percentile rank, because we have no licensed source for one.
- Dose counts are unit-level eMAR aggregates; no patient identifiers, diagnoses or patient-experience responses exist anywhere in the data.
